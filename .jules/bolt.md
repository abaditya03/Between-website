## 2024-10-24 - Canvas Loop Optimization Learning
**Learning:** Modern V8 engines heavily optimize `Math.sqrt`. Micro-optimizing by calculating and comparing squared distances first often doesn't yield significant speedups in JS and may be marginally slower due to assignment overhead. Algorithmic improvements, such as starting the inner nested loop for N-body distance calculations at `j = i + 1` instead of `j = i`, yields a massive execution time improvement. Skipping self-comparisons completely avoids N unnecessary `dist = 0` calculations and 0-length canvas draw calls per frame.
**Action:** When working with nested loops for coordinate or particle interactions, prioritize algorithmic optimizations (like skipping self or reciprocal pair checks) over math micro-optimizations like squared distances.
