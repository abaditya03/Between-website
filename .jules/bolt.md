## 2024-06-02 - Redundant Zero-Length Draw Calls in Particle Networks
**Learning:** In the `ParticleNetwork` component, initializing the inner particle comparison loop at `j = i` instead of `j = i + 1` caused the system to calculate the distance between a particle and itself. This not only resulted in redundant mathematical operations (including `Math.sqrt`) but also issued 0-length draw calls (moveTo and lineTo the exact same coordinates) for every single particle on every single frame.
**Action:** When implementing or reviewing nested loops for object interactions (like particle systems or collision detection), always verify whether self-comparison is necessary or if reciprocal pairs are already skipped, and initialize the inner loop at `j = i + 1` to strictly compare distinct pairs.
